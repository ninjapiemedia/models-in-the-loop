# Models in the Loop — Canonical Brand Guide

This file is the source of truth for future designers, developers, LLMs, and VLMs working on Models in the Loop. Preserve these rules unless the brand owner explicitly changes them.

## Identity
- Master brand: **Models in the Loop**
- Domain: **modelsintheloop.com**
- Brand promise: **Real models. Infinite creative.**
- Brand principle: **The model stays in the loop.**
- Category: **Photography · Motion · Licensing · Generative Media**

## Business idea
Models in the Loop is a human-first media company built around real talent. It combines premium model photography and motion with commercial licensing and permissioned AI-assisted/generative extensions of source content. AI should expand creative possibilities without removing the real people who make the source material possible.

## Logo
The primary symbol is an **ML monogram**. The M incorporates a highly simplified, elegant reclining-model silhouette and the L completes the monogram. It may also subtly evoke machine learning, but the human model remains the primary meaning.

Rules:
- Use solid color only. **No gradients, metallic effects, shadows, bevels, glows, or 3D treatments.**
- The model silhouette must remain abstract, tasteful, minimal, and non-anatomically detailed.
- At small sizes use the ML mark only. Do not force the full wordmark into favicons or social avatars.
- Maintain generous negative space around the mark.
- Preferred applications: Ivory mark on Obsidian; Obsidian mark on Ivory.

## Colors
- **Obsidian** `#0B0B0B` — primary dark / background
- **Ivory** `#F8F1E7` — primary light / logo / typography
- **Nude** `#D9B8A7` — restrained warm accent
- **Sage** `#6B7F6E` — occasional secondary / technology accent

Obsidian + Ivory should represent roughly 90% of visible brand usage. Nude and Sage are accents, not primary fields.

## Voice
Editorial, confident, modern, human-first, premium, commercially literate. Avoid generic AI hype, techno-neon visual clichés, and language implying that models are being replaced.

Preferred language:
- real models / real talent
- permissioned creative
- commercial licensing
- source material
- AI-assisted or generative media
- keep talent in the loop

## Digital UX
- Mobile-first and responsive.
- Minimal navigation and generous whitespace.
- Photography should eventually carry most of the emotional impact.
- Obsidian backgrounds and Ivory typography are the default digital expression.
- Accessibility and legibility take priority over decorative effects.

## Public email architecture
- General: `contact@modelsintheloop.com`
- Collaboration/talent/partnerships: `collab@modelsintheloop.com`
- Licensing: `licensing@modelsintheloop.com`
- Legal/privacy/rights: `legal@modelsintheloop.com`

## Current launch state
Until the full product is ready, the public website is a minimal **Coming Soon** page. It should communicate the brand, category, and contact channels without pretending that unreleased platform features are already available.
