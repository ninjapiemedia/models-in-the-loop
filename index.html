01 / OUR CONCEPT

Made with people.
Not instead of them.

Models in the Loop is a Northwest Arkansas creative studio exploring the intersection of premium photography, motion, commercial licensing, and permissioned AI-assisted media.

We begin with real people, real shoots, and authentic collaboration. Technology can extend creative possibilities, but it never replaces the talent behind the work.

Based in Northwest Arkansas, we connect with adult talent throughout Fayetteville, Bentonville, Rogers, and the surrounding area.

03 / FOR TALENT

Your image.
Your voice.
Your choice.

01

Introduce yourself

Tell us a little about you and share a few recent, unfiltered photos. No professional portfolio required.

02

Let's connect

If there's a potential fit, we'll reach out with information about the creative concept, shoot, and next steps.

03

Agree before we create

Project scope, compensation, usage, licensing, and any AI-assisted use are discussed and agreed in writing before a shoot.

We welcome applicants age 18 and older. All backgrounds and experience levels are encouraged. We are an independent studio, not affiliated with any university or sorority.

04 / APPLY

Let's make something.

Interested in collaborating? Share your details and a few photos. We review inquiries privately and contact selected applicants as opportunities arise.

Applying does not grant permission to publish, license, train AI models on, or generate new images using your likeness. Any such usage requires a separate written agreement.
