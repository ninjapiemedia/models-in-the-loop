PRIVACY

Your privacy matters.

Last updated: October 2026

Information we collect

When you apply, we collect your name, email, phone, city, age, optional social link and introduction, and the photographs you voluntarily submit.

How we use it

We use applications solely to assess potential creative collaborations and to communicate with applicants. Applying does not grant us rights to publish, license, train AI systems on, or generate images from your likeness. Any such use requires a separate written agreement.

Storage and retention

Applications and photographs are stored in restricted-access infrastructure. We aim to delete unsuccessful applications within 90 days of review unless you agree to remain on file. You may request earlier deletion.

Sharing and security

We do not sell application information. Authorized service providers may process it to support storage and email notification. We apply access controls, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

To access, correct, or request deletion of your application, email legal@modelsintheloop.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling requests.
